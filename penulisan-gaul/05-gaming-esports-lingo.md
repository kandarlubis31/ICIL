# 📝 05 — Gaming & Esports Lingo

> 🟡 Intermediate | Prereq: 01 | ~10 min

Indonesia has over 100 million gamers — making gaming slang one of the most widely understood casual registers in the country. Mobile Legends, PUBG Mobile, Valorant, and Free Fire have produced a shared vocabulary that now spills into everyday conversation.

---

## 5.1 Core Gaming Vocabulary

### Social & Team Play

| Term | Origin | Meaning | Non-Gaming Usage |
|------|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Mabar** | Main Bareng | Play together | Any group hangout |
| **Gaskeun** | Gas + Sundanese *-keun* | Let's go! | General enthusiasm |
| **GG / GGWP** | Good Game / Well Played | Match acknowledgment | Respect for any effort |
| **NT** | Nice Try | Consolation after near-win | General encouragement |

### Performance Terms

| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Noob / Newbie** | Inexperienced player | Mild insult or self-deprecation |
| **Beban** | Burden, dead weight | Teammate dragging team down |
| **Bot** | Plays like AI | Stiff, predictable player |
| **AFK** | Away From Keyboard | Inactive, unresponsive |
| **Copong** | Terrible aim | Low-skill player (derogatory) |

### Game Mechanics

| Term | Meaning | Context |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Nerf** | Weaken (character/weapon) | Balance patch reduces power |
| **Buff** | Strengthen | Balance patch increases power |
| **Gank** | Ambush as team | Surround and eliminate |
| **Push Rank** | Climb competitive ladder | Serious competitive play |
| **Meta** | Most Effective Tactic Available | Current optimal strategy |

## 5.3 Writing Gaming Dialogue

### Character Voice Through Slang Density

| Player Type | Slang Profile | Sample Line |
|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Casual mobile** | High Indo slang | "Gaskeun mabar malam ini, push rank yuk!" |
| **Competitive esports** | Mix Indo + English game terms | "Meta sekarang mage jg overpowered, harus di-nerf" |
| **Toxic teammate** | Insult-heavy | "Beban lu, copong, mending AFK aja" |
| **Content creator** | Energetic, family-friendly | "GGWP guys! Keren banget clutch-nya tadi!" |

### Gender Dynamics in Gaming Lingo

Indonesian female gamers often face different slang expectations. Writing female gamer characters requires awareness: *cewe gaming* communities have developed counter-slang (*squad cewe, girl gamer solid*) and in-group terms that differ from male-dominated spaces.

## 5.4 Gaming Slang in Non-Gaming Copy

| Context | Gaming Term | Effect |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Team productivity | "GGWP tim, sprint ini selesai!" | Camaraderie |
| Fitness app | "Gaskeun workout day 30!" | Motivation |
| Marketing CTA | "Push rank karir lo sekarang" | Aspirational |
| Discord community | "Mabar diskusi startup" | Community-building |

**Rule of thumb**: Only use gaming slang if 60%+ of your audience would understand it without explanation.
