# 📝 04 — Digital & Social Media Slang

> 🟡 Intermediate | Prereq: 01 | ~10 min

Indonesian social media slang evolves at breakneck speed — terms born on TikTok this month may be dead by next quarter. This course covers the platform-native vocabulary, viral mechanisms, and writing strategies for staying current without sounding try-hard.

---

## 4.1 Platform-Driven Vocabulary

### TikTok / Instagram Reels Slang

| Term | Origin | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| **FYP** | For You Page | Making it to the algorithm feed |
| **Stecu** | Stelan Cuek | Cold, indifferent aesthetic |
| **Yapping** | English slang | Talking too much, blabbering |
| **Brainrot** | English slang | Low-value addictive content |
| **Angka 67** | Numeric code | Neutral/meh response |
| **POV** | Point of View | Roleplay or perspective framing |
| **Jangan ya dek ya** | Catchphrase | Gentle warning, playful |

### Twitter/X Slang

| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Skena** | Subculture/indie scene | Describing hipster communities |
| **Kalcer** | Culture (phonetic) | Artistic habits, trendy lifestyle |
| **Pick Me** | Attention-seeker | "I'm not like other girls" |
| **Red Flag / Green Flag** | Warning signs / positive traits | Relationship/behavior assessment |
| **Toxic** | Harmful/manipulative | Describing people or environments |

### Lifestyle & Wellness Slang

| Term | Meaning | Context |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Healing** | Vacation/mental break, not medical | "Butuh healing ke Bali" |
| **FOMO** | Fear Of Missing Out | Anxiety about missing trends |
| **JOMO** | Joy Of Missing Out | Happiness from staying home |
| **Self-reward** | Treating yourself | Post-achievement indulgence |
| **Me time** | Personal alone time | Self-care framing |

## 4.2 The Viral Lifecycle

```
┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐
│ Creator  │───▶│ Adoption │───▶│ Saturation│───▶│ Death or  │
│ coins    │    │ by early │    │ (everyone │    │ Dictionary│
│ term     │    │ adopters │    │  uses it) │    │ fossilize │
└──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘
   Week 0          Week 1-2        Week 3-8         Week 8+
```

Writers must identify which stage a term is in:
- **Early adoption**: Edgy, signals insider status. Good for niche audiences.
- **Saturation**: Mainstream-safe. Good for mass-market copy.
- **Death**: Sounds dated. Avoid — unless for ironic/nostalgic effect.

## 4.3 Writing for Platform Context

| Platform | Tone | Slang Density | Example Voice |
|----------|------|---------------|---------------|
| TikTok caption | Hyper-casual, viral-bait | High | "POV: lo abis healing tapi besoknya Senin lagi 💀" |
| Instagram caption | Curated casual | Medium | "JOMO weekend vibes ✨ No FOMO here." |
| Twitter/X | Witty, concise | Medium-High | "red flag kalo doi gak pernah initiate chat 🙃" |
| LinkedIn | Professional-casual | Low | "Interesting insight from the team today" |
| YouTube description | Informative-casual | Low-Medium | "Kita bahas kenapa healing itu penting" |

## 4.4 Meme Language & Intertextuality

Indonesian meme culture layers slang with reference-heavy humor. Effective writers understand:

| Device | Example | Effect |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Template reference** | "Muka lo pas..." + reaction image | Shared cultural knowledge |
| **Sound reference** | Quoting viral audio lines | Instant recognition |
| **Character reference** | "literally me fr fr" | Self-deprecating identification |
| **Numeric humor** | "67" for neutrality | In-group code |
