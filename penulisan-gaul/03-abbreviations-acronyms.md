# 📝 03 — Abbreviations, Portmanteaus & Acronyms

> 🟢 Beginner | Prereq: 01 | ~10 min

Indonesians are world champions of abbreviation. From government agencies (*Pemda*, *Disdik*) to daily emotions (*baper*, *mager*), compression is the default mode of Indonesian communication. This course maps the abbreviation types and teaches you to create authentic-sounding casual text.

---

## 3.1 The Four Types of Compression

| Type | Mechanism | Formal Example | Slang Example |
|------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Initialism** | First letters | *KTP* (Kartu Tanda Penduduk) | *PHP* (Pemberi Harapan Palsu) |
| **Acronym** | Pronounceable initialism | *Tilang* (Bukti Pelanggaran) | *Baper* (Bawa Perasaan) |
| **Portmanteau** | Blended words | — | *Mager* (Malas Gerak) |
| **Truncation** | Cut word | *Fak.* (Fakultas) | *Gak* (Enggak) |

## 3.2 The Essential Abbreviation Lexicon

### Emotion & Mental State

| Term | Expansion | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Baper** | Bawa Perasaan | Overly sensitive, taking things personally |
| **Gabut** | Gaji Buta | Bored, having nothing to do |
| **Mager** | Malas Gerak | Too lazy to move |
| **Galau** | (standalone) | Emotionally confused, heartbroken |
| **Bucin** | Budak Cinta | Love-slave, hopelessly devoted |
| **Jones** | Jomblo Ngenes | Miserable single person |
| **Salfok** | Salah Fokus | Distracted by the wrong thing |
| **Kepo** | Knowing Every Particular Object | Nosy, overly curious |
| **Gaje** | Gak Jelas | Nonsensical, unclear |
| **Garing** | (standalone) | Unfunny, dry (joke) |

### Social & Lifestyle

| Term | Expansion | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Mantul** | Mantap Betul | Really awesome |
| **Nolep** | No Life | Homebody, no social life |
| **Alay** | Anak Layangan | Overly dramatic, flashy |
| **Mokondo** | Modal Kelamin Doang | Freeloader (vulgar, use with caution) |

## 3.3 Abbreviation Patterns

| Pattern | Example | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| First 2 syllables of each word | *Ma-las* + *Ge-rak* → *Mager* | Most common for portmanteaus |
| First phoneme of each word + vowel insert | *Ba-wa Pe-ra-sa-an* → *Baper* | Creates pronounceable acronyms |
| English borrowing abbreviation | *No Life* → *Nolep* | Loanword compression |
| Phrasal compression | *Gak Jelas* → *Gaje* | Two-word → one-word |

## 3.4 Writing Tips

- **Don't over-abbreviate.** Three compressed terms per paragraph max — too many sounds forced.
- **Context first.** *Gabut* works for casual chat, not for a brand tweet about productivity.
- **Beware of regional variance.** *PHP* means something different in Medan vs Jakarta.
- **Test recency.** Abbreviations from 2+ years ago may signal you're out of touch. Check TikTok/Twitter.

## ⚡ Action Checklist

- [ ] Classify any slang abbreviation into its compression type
- [ ] Use emotion-state abbreviations (*baper, gabut, mager*) for character voice
- [ ] Limit to 3 compressed terms per paragraph
- [ ] Verify a term's recency before deploying it in copy
