# 📝 08 — Tone, Voice & Register in Casual Writing

> 🔴 Advanced | Prereq: 01, 02, 03 | ~12 min

The hardest skill in Indonesian casual writing is not knowing the slang — it's knowing when to dial it up, when to pull back, and how to shift registers mid-text without jarring the reader. This course teaches register calibration for professional writers.

---

## 8.1 The Indonesian Register Spectrum

```
FORMAL ←─────────────────────────────────────────────→ SLANG
  │              │              │              │
Bahasa Baku   Semi-formal    Informal     Bahasa Gaul
(KBBI)        (Media)       (Chat)       (Jaksel/Gaming)
  │              │              │              │
Government     News sites    WhatsApp      TikTok
Legal docs     Blogs         DMs           Gaming chat
Academic       Email         Stories       Close friends
```

Most Indonesian professional writing operates in the **Semi-formal → Informal** zone. Pure formal (*bahasa baku*) sounds stiff, pure slang alienates older audiences. The skill is blending.

## 8.2 The Five Voice Dimensions

| Dimension | Scale | Example (Low → High) |
|-----------|-------|---------------------|
| **Warmth** | Cold ↔ Friendly | "Mohon diperhatikan." ↔ "BTW guys, cek ini yuk!" |
| **Formality** | Stiff ↔ Casual | "Dengan hormat..." ↔ "Hai hai!" |
| **Jaksel density** | 0% ↔ 40% English | "Menurut saya..." ↔ "Honestly, menurut gue..." |
| **Compression** | Full words ↔ Abbreviations | "Tidak jelas" ↔ "Gaje" |
| **Playfulness** | Straight ↔ Wordplay | "Baik" ↔ "Sabi lah!" |

## 8.3 Register Shifting Patterns

### The "Hook + Body" Pattern (Content Writing)

```
[HOOK — High warmth, medium slang]
"Guys, ini literally game-changer buat produktivitas lo! 🔥"

[BODY — Medium warmth, low slang]
"Fitur baru ini memungkinkan kamu menyelesaikan task 3x lebih cepat..."

[CTA — High warmth, medium slang]  
"Gaskeun upgrade sekarang! Sabi banget."
```

### The "Formal Frame + Casual Core" (Brand Voice)

```
[INTRO — Semi-formal]
"Kami dengan bangga mengumumkan kolaborasi terbaru..."

[CORE — Informal with Jaksel sprinkles]
"Basically, kita pengen bikin sesuatu yang relate sama Gen Z..."

[OUTRO — Semi-formal]
"Informasi lebih lanjut kunjungi website kami."
```

## 8.4 Audience-Based Register Selection

| Audience | Register | Max Slang Density | Example Terms |
|----------|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Gen Z (13-24) | Hyper-casual | 60% | *stecu, brainrot, gaskeun, mabar* |
| Millennials (25-40) | Casual-professional | 30% | *baper, gabut, healing, which is* |
| Gen X (41-55) | Semi-formal with color | 10% | *healing, FOMO* (widely known) |
| Boomers (56+) | Formal with personality | 5% | Avoid slang unless widely known |
| B2B | Professional-casual | 5% | Only *insight, align, valid* |

## 8.5 The "Cringe" Danger Zone

| Mistake | Why It Cringes | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Brand forcing viral slang | "How do you do, fellow kids?" energy | Wait 4+ weeks after peak virality |
| Mixing eras | Using *alay* + *stecu* same paragraph | Era consistency matters |
| Over-Jaksel | "Which is, basically, literally, actually..." | 1-2 English markers per paragraph |
| Slang in serious context | "Thoughts & prayers 🙏 gaskeun" | Read the room |

## 8.6 The Code-Switch Decision Tree

```
Is the audience under 30?
├─ YES → Can I use Jaksel markers? 
│   ├─ YES → 1-2 English discourse markers per paragraph
│   └─ NO (formal context) → Semi-formal, no abbreviations
└─ NO → Is it a casual platform (TikTok, IG)?
    ├─ YES → Light Jaksel, no abbreviations
    └─ NO → Formal or semi-formal only
```

## ⚡ Action Checklist

- [ ] Map every piece of copy to a register before writing
- [ ] Use "Hook + Body" pattern for content — slang front, substance middle
- [ ] Cap Jaksel density at 40% even for Gen Z audiences
- [ ] Never use slang in serious/delicate contexts (crisis comms, condolences)
