# 📝 Faculty of Casual & Slang Writing — Overview

> **10 Courses** | Beginner → Advanced | Domain: Indonesian informal writing, slang linguistics, code-switching, youth sociolects, register mastery

Bahasa gaul is not broken Indonesian — it is a living subsystem of the language with its own grammar, phonology, and sociology. This faculty trains writers to master casual Indonesian for social media, gaming, marketing, fiction, and authentic youth communication. From Jaksel code-switching to TikTok slang, from gaming lobbies to campus life, these courses cover the full spectrum of Indonesia's informal written voice.
